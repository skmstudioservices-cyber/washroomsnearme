⚡ Quick tips

  • Search Google Maps for "public washroom near me" — it shows distance, opening hours and sometimes photos and reviews.
  • Pay-and-use facilities typically charge a small fee (often ₹5–₹10) and are usually cleaner.
  • Petrol pumps on highways, metro stations, bus stands and malls are the most reliable free-to-use options.
  • Look for Swachh Bharat Mission signage — these are maintained community washrooms.

🗺 The fastest ways to find a washroom nearby

Google Maps is the most reliable tool in India. Open the app or website and search "public washroom near me", "restroom near me" or "Sulabh washroom near me". Results usually include:

  • Distance and walking time from your current location
  • Opening hours (many public washrooms close at night)
  • User ratings and photos — the fastest way to judge cleanliness

If nothing shows up, search for nearby landmarks instead: "petrol pump", "metro station", "railway station", "bus stand", "mall" or "hotel". All of these have restrooms, and most allow non-customers to use them, sometimes for a small fee.

🇮🇳 Public washrooms in India — what to expect

India has expanded public sanitation massively under the Swachh Bharat Mission. You will generally find three kinds of facilities:

1. Free community / public washrooms

Built and maintained by municipal bodies. Free or nominally priced, found near markets, bus stands and slum-adjacent areas. Cleanliness varies — check recent Google Maps reviews.

2. Pay-and-use washrooms (Sulabh and similar)

Operated by NGOs and private agencies — a small fee (typically ₹5, sometimes ₹10) gets you a cleaner, attended facility with water and soap. Sulabh International operates thousands across India.

3. Facility washrooms (malls, metros, petrol pumps)

Often the cleanest and safest option. Malls and metro stations have well-maintained restrooms; highway petrol pumps increasingly have food courts and restrooms.

👩 Women, children & accessibility

  • Many community washrooms have separate women's sections — look for "Mahila" signage or female attendants.
  • Swachh Bharat guidelines require accessible washrooms for persons with disabilities in public facilities.
  • When travelling at night, prefer lit, attended facilities inside petrol pumps, hotels or 24×7 chains rather than dark standalone public washrooms.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How do I find a public washroom near me right now?

Open Google Maps and search 'public washroom near me' or 'restroom near me'. You'll see the nearest options with hours and photos. Petrol pumps, metro stations and malls are reliable fallbacks.

Are public washrooms in India free?

Many community washrooms are free or cost a token amount. Pay-and-use washrooms (like Sulabh facilities) typically charge ₹5–₹10 and are usually cleaner.

Which apps show public washrooms?

Google Maps is the most complete in India. Simply search 'public washroom' or 'restroom' — no special app is needed.

Are there separate washrooms for women?

Yes — most public and pay-and-use washrooms have separate women's sections, often marked 'Mahila', and many have female attendants.
